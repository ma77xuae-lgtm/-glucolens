About GlucoLens

GlucoLens is designed to help users learn about the foods they eat and understand nutrition information.

Food identification is an estimate and may be incorrect. This first version suggests one possible food match. It does not measure blood glucose, identify every ingredient, or calculate exact nutrition from a photo. Always check food labels and follow guidance from your healthcare professional.
